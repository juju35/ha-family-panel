# Changelog

## 1.1.0 - 2026-11-28

### Features
- Tabs at the level of the cards (Pronote, ENT ONE, Family Link, meal, chores, presence and countdown cards).
- Today: tomorrow per child (first and last lesson, canceled lessons), **To handle** with its action (tick a chore or homework, sign a word of the liaison book, read the school's messages, +15 min of screen time), who's home (zone, distance and direction, phone battery), tonight's dinner with its servings, birthdays and the next countdowns.
- School: today's and the next timetable (lesson going on, canceled and changed lessons), homework by day ticked through ENT ONE, words to sign, latest grades with the class average, min and max, averages with the class marker, past periods, absences, late arrivals, punishments, the canteen's menu and the school's news.
- Meals: tonight with its time and servings, the week as plates, planning or changing a day in Mealie (recipe, note, random, clear), the shopping list by aisle.
- Chores: a filter per person, overdue first, avatars and points, each child's points.
- Screens: status, the week, top apps and their rules, devices with place and battery, bedtime and school time, school mode, +15 / +30 min / +1 h, revoke bonus, lock, ring and locate.
- Signing a word and locking a phone are confirmed in the panel's own dialog; a planned meal is changed in place in Mealie (update, or delete then plan again), never given a second meal in its slot.

## 1.0.0 - 2026-11-27

### Features
- **Family panel** in the sidebar, at `/family`, with an illustrated 90 px top bar (a house, day or night with the theme) and seven tabs: Today, School, Agenda, Meals, Chores, Screens and Settings.
- **Today**: one card per child (homework, things to sign, unread messages, average, chores done), what needs handling, tomorrow's school day and first lesson, tonight's bin, tonight's and tomorrow's meals, the day's events, screen time and countdowns.
- **School**: each child's lessons of tomorrow, homework to do, averages and school life, from Pronote, ENT ONE, Educartable or Skolengo.
- **Agenda**: the family calendars over a week, one colour per calendar.
- **Meals**: the week's meal plan (Mealie, Cookidoo or any calendar) and the shopping list, with items to tick off or add.
- **Chores**: the chore lists (Donetick, Grocy or any to-do list), done, due or overdue.
- **Screens**: each child's Family Link screen time, limit, bedtime and blocked apps, with +15 min, lock and ring buttons.
- **Settings**: the sources found, the header picture (auto, day, night) and an administrators-only switch.
- Sources found by integration, or chosen in the integration's options; children merged by first name across the school integrations and Family Link.
- 22 languages.
