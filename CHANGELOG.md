# Changelog

## 1.0.0 - 2026-11-27

### Features
- **Family panel** in the sidebar, at `/family`, with an illustrated 90 px top bar (a house, day or night with the theme) and seven tabs: Today, School, Agenda, Meals, Chores, Screens and Settings.
- **Today**: one card per child (homework, things to sign, unread messages, average, chores done), what needs handling, tomorrow's school day and first lesson, tonight's bin, tonight's and tomorrow's meals, the day's events, screen time and countdowns.
- **School**: each child's lessons of tomorrow, homework to do, averages and school life, from Pronote, ENT ONE, Educartable or Skolengo.
- **Agenda**: the family calendars over a week, one colour per calendar.
- **Meals**: the week's meal plan (Mealie, Cookidoo or any calendar) and the shopping list, with items to tick off or add.
- **Chores**: the chore lists (Donetick, Grocy or any to-do list), done, due or overdue.
- **Screens**: each child's Family Link screen time, limit, bedtime and blocked apps, with +15 min, lock and ring buttons.
- **Settings**: the sources found, the header picture (auto, day, night) and an administrators-only switch.
- Sources found by integration, or chosen in the integration's options; children merged by first name across the school integrations and Family Link.
- 22 languages.
